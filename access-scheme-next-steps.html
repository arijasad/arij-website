After your application.

If you have just submitted your application, I’ll review your details and reply by email about availability. Your interview is not booked yet.

Arrange teacher confirmation

Ask any teacher at your current school or college to email arijasadmaths@gmail.com from their official school email address, confirming your full name and current enrolment.

If your teacher has already sent confirmation, there is no need to send it again. No reference, proof of household income or additional documents are required.

Your booking will only be confirmed once verification is received and a time is agreed, subject to availability. Each eligible student may receive one free mock interview, with written feedback and recommendations.

For any questions about your application, use the email address above.
