Free Maths Teaching Resources: Questions and Solutions

Choose a problem, listen to the reasoning, and use the next question to work on the difficulty you find.

By Arij Asad27 October 2026

Questions and solutions ready for a lesson

Download the teaching pack (ZIP)

The pack brings together my existing logic, algebra and textbook practice sheets. PDFs are ready to print or send to students. The TeX files can be uploaded to Overleaf or used to select problems for a lesson.

You may copy and adapt my original questions for your lessons and share them with your students. Please keep the Arij Asad Maths credit and source link. Official papers and third-party textbook solutions remain on their original websites.

What is included

  • Negation and contrapositive: separate question and question-with-solution sheets, each in PDF and TeX.
  • Algebra diagnostic: student questions, teacher solutions and follow-up practice, each in PDF and TeX.
  • Six original textbook practice sheets: Pure Years 1 and 2, Statistics and Mechanics Years 1 and 2, and Core Pure 1 and 2. Each PDF includes questions followed by hints and solutions, with an editable TeX source.

For the textbook sheets, print or send only the question pages if you want to save the hints and solutions for discussion. The pack’s START_HERE file explains the folders.

Use the sheet to find the next question

  1. Choose a question that reveals a particular skill. Let the student explain how they would start.
  2. When progress stops, identify the difficulty. Are they missing a fact, overlooking a condition, or unsure which approach to try?
  3. Offer one useful hint and give them time to act on it.
  4. Ask why the method works, then change part of the problem and try again.
  5. Return to the original question later without the hint. Use the result to decide what to practise next.

The teaching practice sequences show how to connect these skills to longer problems. For spoken reasoning, use the interview questions and follow-ups; for written arguments, try the BMO1 proof-writing guide.
