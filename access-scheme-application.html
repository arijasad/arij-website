State School Access Scheme

Apply for a free mock interview.

For eligible students attending UK state-funded schools and sixth-form colleges. You can apply now and arrange teacher confirmation afterwards.

The access scheme is intended primarily for students attending non-selective state schools and sixth-form colleges who have limited access to specialist Oxbridge interview preparation.

Students attending specialist mathematics schools are not eligible for free mock interviews under this scheme.

All five fields are required. No payment details or supporting documents are needed.

I’ll use this address to arrange the interview and send feedback.

If undecided, write “Not yet decided”.

Your booking will only be confirmed once teacher verification is received and a time is agreed, subject to availability.

Your application is sent through the existing enquiry service, FormSubmit, to my tuition inbox. If the student is under 18, a parent or guardian should normally submit the form. Please use only the details requested above. Privacy notice.

Teacher confirmation

Ask any teacher at your current school or college to email arijasadmaths@gmail.com from their official school email address, confirming your full name and current enrolment.

No formal reference, proof of household income or additional supporting documents are required.

You do not need to wait for your teacher’s email before applying.

Eligible new, existing and previous students are welcome, including students who have paid for sessions before. Each eligible student may receive one free interview, subject to availability.

No obligation to purchase additional sessions.
