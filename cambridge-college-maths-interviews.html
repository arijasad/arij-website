How to use this guide.

What if two official pages disagree?

Follow the instructions in your interview invitation. If anything is still unclear, contact the college’s admissions team.

How should I prepare for an online interview?

Read your college’s platform and equipment instructions. Practise writing mathematics, sharing a diagram and explaining your work using the same setup. Ask the college early if you cannot access the required equipment.
